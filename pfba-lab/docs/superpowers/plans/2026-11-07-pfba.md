## Revised objective approved in the conversation

- Engineering owner: independent decoupling subagent, with prediction and translation overlap on separately assigned devices, causal arrival, and queue/backpressure verification. Compare batch TAF, exact-request reuse, naive KV, latest-arrived scheduling, and decoupled scheduling as separate ablations.
- Acceptance: lock the operating point on development data, then require absolute mean LAAL difference at most 0.5 source words on an independent test set. Require paired 95% confidence intervals for both corpus BLEU and mean COMET improvements to exclude zero, and require a paired reduction in wall-clock time. Report the first output, sentence completion, and p95. Count actual GPU work, avoid calling replay time a live speech measurement, and identify the input clock.
- Quality: engineering-only changes with the same inputs/decisions should preserve outputs. Seek quality improvement through a learned selection or commit policy that predicts stable early translation and penalizes incorrect commits. Compare with the strongest fixed budget found on dev.
- Neural controller: add a small multilayer perceptron for action-conditioned confidence with sentence-grouped calibration, sharing features, utility/cost modeling, and held-out evaluation with the logistic controller.
- No final test reuse for tuning. The current 12-sentence official test run is a feasibility pilot. Reserve a disjoint untouched subset for the final acceptance run.
